# Press outlet logos

Seat pictures for the International Press Corps preset, drawn through `src/lib/pressOutlets.ts`. Each file is a 256 px square PNG: the logo from Wikimedia Commons, fitted to the largest box that sits inside the seat circle, on white. Al Jazeera and Reuters use their emblem only (their wordmark is too thin to read at seat size).

The marks are trademarks of their owners. They are used only to identify the outlet a delegate represents in a simulation, not to suggest any endorsement. Xinhua News Agency has no freely licensed logo on Commons, so its seat shows initials.
