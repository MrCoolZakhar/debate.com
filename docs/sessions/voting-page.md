## FEATURE: VOTING PAGE (/voting/[code])

- Separate page from main session, opened from the Vote button beside the Documents tabs (carries `?chairName=`)
- Shows delegates one by one: In Favour / In Favour with Rights / Abstain / Pass / Against with Rights / Against. **There is no pass round** (17 Sep 2026): a delegation that passes is asked once more at the end of the line (see "The line" below)
- **Ballot choices are settings** (17 Sep 2026): `allowRightsVotes` and `allowPass` on `CommitteeSettings`, both default true, key-level patches (`applyRules` on this page, `upd` in Settings). Controls: Settings → Voting (one small row under the threshold, `VotingTab.tsx`) and the roll call's Abstain bookmark ("Ballot choices"). Off hides the rights buttons / the Pass button on the ballot. Rights off also means no rights speakers, and a device reveal counts a rights choice cast before the switch as the plain vote. Device ballots: `DeviceBallotScreen` hides the rights options from `committee.dbSettings`, and `cast_device_vote` refuses them with `no_rights` (migration `device_vote_respects_allow_rights`). A device ballot has no Pass anyway
- **When the last delegation in the line has voted, the vote finishes on its own** (owner: "automatically move to rights speakers"): `advance` goes straight to the rights speakers when anyone voted with rights (and rights are allowed), otherwise straight to the result with `persistResult`. A roll-call ballot has no all-voted screen any more; it remains only for a device ballot after Back, and for a ballot stored earlier that sits at the end of its line
- Rights speakers handled in sequence after all votes
- P5 veto mode: one P5 Against = failed
- Custom veto mode (`vetoMode: 'custom'`): one Against from any delegation in `vetoCountries` = failed. Matched by country identity (`src/lib/vetoMatch.ts`, re-exported by `VotingRulesPanel`), the same `vetoListFor` → `evaluate` path as P5 (`vetoListFor` / `holdsVeto` live in `src/components/voting/vetoHolders.ts` since 18 Sep 2026)
- Unanimous mode: all P+V must vote For
- Result shown with pass/fail, vote counts; "Vote Again" opens the roll call for the same paper, and confirming it re-freezes the room and starts a new ballot

### Back (replaces "Correct a vote", 15 Sep 2026)
- `stepBack()` is one `updateVote`, so `seq` grows and `vote_state` stays authoritative. Result or rights speakers at the first speaker → the ballot at its end, votes kept, `result` null, and the paper's `documents.status` put back to `introduced` through `persistResult` until the vote is finished again. That status write waits for Back's own vote save to land (`backStatusSeqRef` + `flushBackStatus`, including a Retry after a refusal); if the save comes back `stale` and another device's ballot wins, `introduced` is never written and the stored state is applied instead, and a new verdict before the save lands cancels it; rights speakers → previous rights speaker; ballot → `currentVoterIndex - 1` along the line (a second ask included; nothing is removed, the recorded choice is shown). From the result or the first rights speaker a roll-call ballot lands on the LAST seat of the line with its choice shown (Keep and continue finishes the vote again); a device ballot lands on its all-voted screen. Repeatable back to the first voter.
- After Back the delegation on screen shows its recorded choice (`Recorded: …`, or a neutral line while the tally is hidden) with the matching ballot button ringed; **Keep and continue** (`keepAndAdvance`) moves on without changing it, any ballot button replaces it.
- **The chair moves back AND forward along the line (17 Sep 2026 backwards; forwards 13 Oct 2026, owner: "I can click countries to go back but not forward, once a country has voted").** `VoterCarousel` takes `onPick(index)` and `canPick(index)`: a seat is a button (`voting_go_back_to` behind the one on screen, `voting_go_to` ahead) when it is DONE (a main-ballot seat with a vote or a Pass, a second-ask seat with a vote: `lineSeatDone` in the page) or is the FRONTIER, the first seat not done (`firstOpenSeat`); never beyond the frontier, so nobody is skipped, and never the invisible outermost step. Every seat with something on record shows its mark, ahead of the current one too. Two arrows sit beside the line (`voting_prev_country` / `voting_next_country`, ChevronLeft / ChevronRight, mirrored in Arabic): Previous = the seat before; Next = the next seat when it is done, else the frontier, disabled when there is nowhere to go. Left / Right do the same (`BallotKeyNav`), never while a text field has focus, the roll call is open or a dialog is over the page. `goToVoter(index)` is ONE `updateVote` setting `currentVoterIndex`, so the seq grows and every device follows, and it never recomputes the position: the seat arrives with its recorded choice shown, which can be changed or kept (Keep and continue). Recording a vote or a Pass moves to the next seat that is not done (`firstOpenSeat` from the next position), so the delegations ahead that already voted are not asked again. Moderator only, ballot only (status `voting`), never on a device ballot. The carousel is no longer `aria-hidden` as a whole: each non-pickable seat carries its own `aria-hidden` and `pointer-events: none`, so the line is still silent to a screen reader except for the flags that do something.

### The line (replaces the pass round, 17 Sep 2026)
- Owner: "Do not have a pass round, they simply vote next." The ballot is ONE line: `order` (frozen), then `passedIds` in the order the delegations passed. `currentVoterIndex` points into it: below `order.length` is the main ballot, at or above it is `passedIds[index - order.length]`, asked again once. Every step goes through `advance(make)`, one `updateVote`; walking past the end finishes the vote (see above).
- **The pass rule (chosen; the usual MUN rule):** a delegation that passes is asked again at the end and must then vote For or Against, with rights when rights are allowed. On the second ask there is no Pass and no Abstain (`voting_again_rule`). A delegation can pass once: Pass exists in the main ballot only. The main ballot shows "N to vote again at the end" (`voting_passed_waiting`); the second ask shows "Asked again · n of N" (`voting_again_title`, stage `voting_stage_again`).
- Invariants: in the main ballot a delegation has a vote OR a Pass, never both (`castVoteAndAdvance` in the main ballot drops the Pass, and with it the second ask; `handlePass` drops the vote). A delegation that passed and was asked again keeps its place in `passedIds` AND holds a vote: its main-ballot seat reads Pass, its second-ask seat reads the vote. Commenters get none of these controls and every writer returns on `isViewOnly`.
- **Ballots stored before:** the old pass round kept `passedIds` in ballot order and moved the same pointer past `order.length` once per pass-round vote, so the index arithmetic is identical and they load unchanged. The one odd case is a state where the old Back was used in the pass round (it removed a vote and set the pointer to `order.length`): the chair sees the first returning delegation with its choice recorded and presses Keep and continue. Nothing is lost.

### The vote is persisted (`documents.vote_state`, `src/lib/voteState.ts`)
- One jsonb object per draft resolution: `status` (voting | rights-speakers | result), the frozen `order` (ballot order, also the P/PV numerator) and `votable` (quorum/veto denominator) as `{id, country}`, `votes`, `currentVoterIndex`, `passedIds`, `rightsOrder`, `rightsIndex`, `rightsTimerLimit`, `rightsTimes` (optional per-speaker seconds), `result`, `vetoed` (optional: the failure came from a veto holder voting against; set with the verdict by `verdictOf` on every finish path and when a rules change re-evaluates a result), `seq`. A column rather than a table: one live vote per document, and `documents` already has the right RLS (`sess_upd`), realtime publication and subscriptions. Cost: every ballot write fires a `documents` event. Every surface's session sync coalesces those (200 ms) and fetches only the documents slice; the voting page adds one light `loadVoteStates` read. `updatedAt` / `startedAt` are stamped with `serverNowIso()` because follower devices sort open votes by them.
- Written ONLY by `saveVoteState` → `save_document_vote_state(p_document, p_state)` (SECURITY INVOKER). `seq` must strictly grow (compared as numeric, so no cast can throw), so a stale device cannot overwrite a newer state; it returns `'ok' | 'stale' | 'denied'`, and an invalid state (not an object, a fractional or out-of-range seq) is `'denied'`, never an exception. The page writes optimistically (a ref plus state), then serialises the saves; a refused save keeps the ballot on screen with a Retry. A `stale` save re-reads: the SAME ballot (same `startedAt`) is re-applied under a seq above the stored one; a different ballot means the DB wins and the stored state is loaded.
- **The verdict write is checked (V2).** `persistResult` → `updateDocumentStatus` (now `Promise<boolean>`, row-counted); a refused write shows `voting_save_failed` with Retry. The document list treats `stored?.status === 'result'` as voted even when `documents.status` was lost (and re-records it on open), so "start a vote" is never offered over a stored result. The Moderator can reopen a voted paper that has a stored state and use Back to correct a placard after a reload. `stepBack`, `finishWithResult` and every ballot write refuse on a view-only device.
- **One device drives**: the Moderator's, by the same `deriveGavelRole(committee, chairName, getGavelDeviceId(code))` as the chair page. Every other chair device is view-only (UI gate, rule 15): no vote buttons, no roll call modal, no rules changes (`VotingRulesPanel readOnly`), no End Debate, no rights clock; it follows the open vote live (`followLive`) and can look at any stored vote.
- A reload never loses a ballot: the document list offers **Resume vote** for an unfinished one and the roll call modal stays closed while a vote is open.
- **Correcting one placard**: Back (see above). The "Correct a vote" list and `correctVote` are gone.
- The rights countdown is local to the driving device (never written per second).

### The room's phase (`set_committee_voting_phase`)
- Opening the page as the Moderator calls `setVotingPhase(id, true)`: in ONE statement it stores the current phase in `settings.votingReturnPhase` and sets `phase = 'voting'`, so delegate phones show "Vote in progress" and Request to Speak closes. In the same transaction it **pauses every clock** (V1): a running caucus total is frozen at its live value (the `pauseCaucusLive` math: a moderated total read at min(now, the speaker clock's zero)) and a running `current_speaker` clock is paused in one update at its live remaining, so a vote never drains a speaker or a caucus. The dais presses play after returning. Already voting = no-op (a reload never overwrites the remembered phase). A suspended or ended room is refused (`voting_phase_closed`); the entry effect is keyed on whether the room is closed, so it is attempted again the moment the room resumes.
- "Back to Session" (Moderator) calls `setVotingPhase(id, false)`: restores exactly the remembered phase; a remembered caucus phase whose `caucus` is gone falls back to the GSL; nothing remembered falls back to the caucus on the row, else the GSL; not voting = no-op.
