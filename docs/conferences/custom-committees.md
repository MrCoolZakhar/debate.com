## 5c. Custom (parliamentary) committees

`committee_type = 'custom'` is the fourth type: seats are members of groups (political groups, parties, benches) rather than countries. Groups live in `conference_committees.groups` (jsonb), a seat's group in `committee_country_slots.group_id`, and a seat or a group can carry a crest (`logo_url`). `src/lib/slotGroups.ts` is the contract: `effectiveSlotArt` decides what a seat draws (own crest, group crest, national flag, fallback), `loadSlotArtIndex` serves surfaces that render many seats, and `PARLIAMENT_PRESETS` seeds the usual chambers. Every flag renderer that matters goes through `FlagImg` or the assignment board's `CountryFlag`, both of which accept `logoUrl`. Debate, allocation and sessions are unchanged; only the seat's identity and picture differ.

**Set-up (12 Oct 2026, owner).** The type cards read MUN (was General Assembly), Crisis, Custom (Specialised merged in: a stored `specialised` row keeps its type and country list but reads as Custom everywhere; it gets no groups) and Press. A custom committee's name field suggests `PARLIAMENT_PRESETS` as you type (`ConferenceCommitteeNameInput onParliamentSelect`: Model European Parliament, UK Commons, US Congress, Lok Sabha, Bundestag, Canada, Australia, Government vs Opposition, Left to Right); picking one sets the name and APPENDS its groups (party colour + `short_name`, kept inside the `groups` jsonb, no schema change). Groups are listed ONCE, as compact chips (disc, short name, seat count; full name in the tooltip) capped at 132px with their own scroll, above a seat list that shows only Unassigned seats; a seat is dragged onto a chip (pointer events, mouse from the row, touch from the grip; "Move to group" is the keyboard path). A chip opens `parliamentGroupPopup.tsx` in the committee editor's own look (two columns: name, short name, colour, crest, add named seats, remove group | the group's seats and Unassigned to move in). No numbered seats.

**Press committees (12 Oct 2026, owner's first draft; SQL applied the same day as migration `press_committees`, verified: an accepted observer sees 7 rooms and is refused a release).** `committee_type = 'press'` is a fifth type (International Press Corps): its seats are outlets or journalists, free text like Crisis (`rosterModeOf` → character, `seatNounsOf` → outlet), offered as a Newspaper emoji card (`parliamentSetup.tsx`) ONLY once the SQL is live (`usePressCommitteesAvailable`, `src/lib/press.ts`, probes `my_press_access`). Allocation, applications and its live room are those of any committee. Its seat holders, its chairs and the conference's accepted / assigned / checked-in observers get the **Press Desk**, `/conferences/[slug]/press` (noindex), linked from the You tab (`participant/PressDeskEntry.tsx`, draws nothing for anyone else): a read-only card per committee room (emblem, mode, speaker and the next 3, motions on the floor, papers introduced or later readable through `PdfViewer` or their text), re-read every 10 s while visible. Never chat, feedback, scores, notes, votes or `committees.settings`. Press seats and press chairs (not observers) can send a **press release** (600 characters, an optional photo in the broadcast image path, 3 in any 24 hours): one `session_broadcasts` row per open room with `source = 'press'` and `author_label` (the outlet), shown in every room's Everyone chat as "Press · BBC" and never as a chair card. No chat or DMs with delegates (owner). Everything is in `scratch-press.sql` (type CHECK, the two columns and a tightened organiser insert policy, `press_releases`, `_press_access`, `my_press_access`, `press_desk`, `send_press_release`, a rolled-back test): **not applied**; apply it only after the client that reads `source` is deployed, or older bundles will show releases as Secretariat messages. Until then the desk says it is not open, the You tab shows nothing and the type card is hidden.
