## 3. Business model (as implemented, not as pitched)

| Mechanism | Where |
|---|---|
| **Credits**: 1 credit = 1 application (delegate, head-delegate, faculty-advisor, observer; chairs exempt). USD 1 each, bundles from 10 up discounted by the server's tiers (see "Buying credits and Unlimited" below). Bought in the credits pop-up (`create-credit-checkout` v5, embedded Stripe Checkout); refunded on rejection. Welcome credit + one-time modal on signup (`CreditsWelcomeGate`, `src/components/CreditsWelcomeModal.tsx`; prompt 134: "Introducing Credits", "Credits are how you apply and unlock things on Gavelling. One balance for everything", five rows: Apply to conferences (1 credit per conference, however many times you edit, refunded if not accepted), Unlock premium MUN guides, Bring your delegation (the delegation pool), Run your conference (the Store), No processing fees; the gold-tinted line "Your first credit is on us. Apply to a conference today!", "Explore conferences" and "Maybe later"). | `src/lib/creditPricing.ts`, `src/lib/purchasePopup.ts`, `src/hooks/useCredits.ts` |
| **Subscriptions**: Unlimited (unlimited credits): USD 3 a month or USD 30 a year, the same price everywhere, no regions. Pro is hidden from the UI and not sold. Whether someone is on Unlimited is `my_unlimited_status()` (a personal `unlimited_*` subscription, active or trialing, not past `current_period_end`), read client-side through `useUnlimitedStatus()` (`src/lib/unlimitedStatus.ts`). `profiles.unlimited_status` is DEAD: nothing ever wrote it (every profile read `none` while 129 subscriptions were live); never read it. **Unlimited Forever (27 Sep 2026):** a permanent plan (ambassadors, `ambassador_permanent_<user_id>` rows: `unlimited_yearly`, active, `current_period_end` NULL) reads as "Unlimited Forever". `my_unlimited_status()` answers `annual` with no end date, no Stripe, no renewal and no cancel; `parseUnlimitedDetail` derives `forever` once (and `useMySubscription().forever`), and the Subscription page, the Unlimited pop-up and every date line show no date, trial, renewal, cancel or Get Unlimited for it. Badges read "Unlimited". | `src/app/account/unlimited/page.tsx`, `payments.ts` |
| **Conference fees**: **there is NO platform fee** (owner, 23 Sep 2026; Stripe Connect is unavailable in many countries, so manual payment is a first-class path, not a fallback). Card payments are direct charges on the organiser's Stripe Connect account (or, for `platform_collects` conferences, the platform account) with no `application_fee_amount`; the participant pays exactly the invoice amount, recomputed server-side in the `create-checkout` edge function. `PLATFORM_FEE_RATE` / `PROCESSING_RATE` in `src/lib/finance.ts` are legacy: `computeCheckout` still computes a `serviceFee`, but no screen shows it and nothing charges it. Do not wire them back in. Manual payments with proof review sit beside Stripe. | `src/lib/finance.ts`, `manage/[slug]/financials/*` |
| **Gavelling Points (retired 23 Sep 2026)**: no longer earned or shown. The welcome-points trigger, `grant_welcome_points()`, `award_points_for()` and the points block in `publish_conference_awards()` were dropped. `points_ledger` and `profiles.points_balance` are kept as history only; never write them and never build on them. | `points_ledger` (history only) |

**Money shown to anyone is the payments ledger, never `payment_status`** (19 Sep 2026). `conference_money_summary(conference, detail)` (`src/lib/conferenceMoney.ts`) is the one definition used by the dashboard money card, Financials and /admin: *received* = succeeded Stripe payments (money that came in through Gavelling), *offline* = succeeded manual payments (organiser mark-paid / approved proof), shown apart and never added, *outstanding* = open invoice balances of accepted participants. `payment_status = 'paid'` is an access flag: free registrations (chairs) are stamped paid on arrival and pledge-covered members are paid with no money of their own, so never multiply it by a fee. The daily report and `admin_platform_metrics` use the same split.

Hard rules: organisers are never charged; Unlimited status is server-verified; public price display goes through `displayDelegatePrice` / `fetchDelegatePrices` in `src/lib/publicFees.ts` (reading the read-only `conference_public_fees` view), because `conferences.fee_amount` is a stale denormalised column: "TBD" and no Pricing details list until delegate applications are set up (delegate role config `is_enabled`, open now or opening later); once set up, the delegate price of the current fee stage (for an upcoming opening, the stage that applies at opening) with the Pricing details list ("Applications open {date}" when upcoming), "Free" at 0. The creation wizard asks no price. **The Registration Fee on the conference page (prompt 117):** `public_registration_fee(p_conference_id)` (signed in or out) answers null or `{label, amount_cents, currency, applies_to}`; the page reads it once and shows "+ {label}" in the muted colour under the price medallion (above, and apart from, the Credit sponsored mark) and a last Pricing details row with the amount and "Per delegation or independent delegate" or "Per delegate".

**Buying credits and Unlimited (24 Sep 2026): two pop-ups, one price source, no page change.**
- **Prices come from the database and nowhere else.** `credit_price_tiers()` (anon + authenticated) returns `{unit_cents, max_qty, tiers:[{min_qty,pct}]}` (100 cents, 500 max; 10+ 10%, 25+ 15%, 50+ 20%, 100+ 25%), and `credit_price_cents(qty)` / `credit_discount_pct(qty)` are the exact charge. The client reads the table ONCE (`src/lib/creditPricing.ts`: `useCreditPriceTable`, `priceCentsFor`, `discountPctFor`, `formatUsd`) and computes with the same rounding, `round(qty * unit * (100 - pct) / 100)`, so a chip, a total and Stripe's amount cannot disagree (12 credits = $10.80). Never type a price or a percentage into the client; until the table loads the UI shows "…". `create-credit-checkout` (v5) takes no price from the client. Unlimited is flat (USD 3 a month, USD 30 a year, `unlimitedPricing()` in `payments.ts` for display; `create-subscription-checkout` v10 is the truth).
- **The pop-ups.** `src/lib/purchasePopup.ts` is the store (`openCreditsPopup({context, conferenceName?, preselect?, onComplete?})`, `openUnlimitedPopup({renewOnce?, onComplete?})`, `closePurchasePopup()`, the two swap into each other keeping `onComplete`); `src/components/purchase/*` renders them (`PurchasePopupHost` in the root layout, mounted BEFORE `AuthModalHost` so a sign-in prompt raised from inside one lands on top). Credits is the light pop-up in the login kit's language; Unlimited is dark forest and gold on purpose. Contexts and bundles (`CREDIT_BUNDLES`): header / pricing / manage 1, 10, 25, 50, 100; apply 1, 5, 10, 20, 25 (1 preselected, the conference named); pay 1, 5, 10, 25, 50; organizer 25, 50, 100, 200; "Another amount" up to `max_qty`. No Credits/Unlimited toggle at the top, no "USD 1 a credit", no "never expires" (owner). Credits are ALWAYS the signed-in person's own balance, whoever buys: nothing sends `societyId` any more (`DelegationCreditsCard` shows the personal balance; an existing society pool is only read).
- **Cancel and resume inside Gavelling (25 Sep 2026).** Manage account's plan card (`src/app/account/manage/subscription/page.tsx`) shows exactly one state from the whole `my_unlimited_status()` object (`src/lib/subscriptionManage.ts`, `useUnlimitedDetail`; the RPC now also returns `on_stripe`, `renews`, `cancel_at_period_end`, `can_cancel`, `can_resume`, `lapsed_plan`, `lapsed_at`): renewing ("Renews on {date}", "Update payment method" opens the Stripe portal, where card changes stay, and a quiet "Cancel subscription"), cancelled ("We hate to see you go" with Gavin, `SAD_GAVIN_SRC`, until Peter's sad drawing lands; "Unlimited stays on until {date}"; the Resume Subscription card), trial (ends on {date}, renews nothing, nothing to cancel), the one-time year ("Paid once. Active until {date}. Nothing renews."), the failed renewal (unchanged, RENEW FOR A YEAR), nothing ("Get Unlimited", or "Come Back to Unlimited" when a paid plan lapsed in the last 180 days). Cancelling opens `CancelUnlimitedSheet` ("Cancel Unlimited?", optional reason chips: too_expensive, no_conferences_soon, missing_feature, payment_trouble, just_trying, other with a 500-character note; "Keep Unlimited" is primary) and calls the edge function `manage-subscription` (`{action:'cancel', reasons?, note?}` or `{action:'resume'}`, the user's JWT, `{ok, status}` or `{ok:false, error}` shown as written, safe to repeat), which cancels AT PERIOD END and records `subscription_cancellations`; resume works while the period still runs. After either action the client RE-READS `my_unlimited_status()` (`notifyPlanChanged`) and never trusts local state; the Stripe webhook (v17) syncs `cancel_at_period_end`, so a cancel made in the Stripe portal shows up too. The Unlimited pop-up opens in RESUME mode wherever it is opened from while `can_resume` is true ("Resume Unlimited", the four benefits highlighted, one "Resume subscription" button, no checkout; success closes it and runs the caller's `onComplete` like a purchase), and reads "Come Back to Unlimited" after a lapse. Trials and one-time years renew nothing and offer no cancel. `/admin` → Data carries a Cancellations card (`admin_unlimited_cancellations(30)`: total, resumed, reasons as bars in the chips' wording, the ten latest notes).
- **Payment happens inside the pop-up.** `@stripe/react-stripe-js` Embedded Checkout with `NEXT_PUBLIC_STRIPE_PUBLISHABLE_KEY` (`src/components/purchase/StripeEmbedded.tsx`); the edge function is called with `embedded: true` off a token read fresh at call time (`src/lib/purchaseCheckout.ts`, never a token captured in React state). Pressing Pay mints the session, then Stripe's form (with its own "Pay $X") replaces the bundle grid, with a "Change" link back. Without the key the same button sends the person to the hosted page. `onComplete` closes the pop-up, toasts (`notifyOk`, the stack is mounted lazily by the host on pages without their own), and `pollCreditsUntilChanged()` (`useCredits.ts`) re-reads the balance until the webhook lands, then `refreshCreditsEverywhere()` updates every mounted reader. **The plan is ONE store (26 Sep 2026):** `unlimitedStatus.ts` caches the whole `my_unlimited_status()` object per user; `useUnlimitedStatus()` (yes/no), `useUnlimitedDetail()` (also re-exported from `subscriptionManage.ts`), `useMySubscription()` and the premium guides' access all re-read on `notifyUnlimitedChanged()` (= `notifyPlanChanged`), so the account rail, the profile menu, Manage account, the pop-ups and guides can no longer disagree (the owner paid and the rail said Unlimited while the page beside it said Free). After an Unlimited payment (embedded completion or the `?unlimited=success` return) `waitForUnlimited()` polls the plan, and after a credit payment `pollCreditsUntilChanged()` polls the balance, on one schedule (`src/lib/purchaseActivation.ts`: every 1.5 s for 30 s, then every 10 s for 2 more minutes); `PurchaseActivationNotice` (mounted by the host) shows "Activating Unlimited" / "Adding your credits" top right and, past 30 s, "Your payment went through. Unlimited can take a minute to switch on; this page will update by itself." When the cancel lands, Manage account → Subscription opens "We hate to see you go..." (`purchase/CancelledPopup.tsx`: until when it stays on, a link to /about (contact), "Resume subscription", Gavin large on the right). A 3-D Secure payment returns through `returnTo?credits=success&session_id=…` / `?unlimited=success&…`, which `PurchaseReturnHandler` (in the host) handles on ANY page: toast, refresh, strip the params. A 409 from the subscription function means Unlimited is already active and is shown as a sentence. `renew: false` is sent from exactly one place: `/pricing/subscription?renew=once`, the failed-renewal email's link, which opens the Unlimited pop-up in "Pay once for a year" mode (Manage account → Subscription offers RENEW FOR A YEAR when the latest row is `past_due`, the only in-app way there). Stripe retired `ui_mode: 'embedded'` on 25 Sep 2026: `create-credit-checkout` v6 and `create-subscription-checkout` v11 use `embedded_page`; the client flow is unchanged. Stripe's own colours inside the form (its Pay button) come from the Stripe Dashboard branding, never from code. Buying while on a trial or promo Unlimited is allowed (`useMySubscription()` in `src/lib/mySubscription.ts` tells trial / paid / past_due / lapsed and the end date; the webhook adds the remaining days); only an active PAID plan is refused.

**Stripe's own limits are the thing that breaks a big bill, and they live only in
the edge function.** `create-checkout` (v18, 8 Sep 2026; not in git, read it with

long invoice list hit them in production:

- A metadata VALUE is capped at 500 characters. `metadata[invoice_ids]` used to be
  one comma-joined list of UUIDs, which overflows at the 14th invoice and makes
  Stripe reject the whole session, so the participant simply cannot pay.
  `setIdListMetadata()` now spreads them over `invoice_ids`, `invoice_ids_2`, ...
  plus `invoice_ids_count`. **Nothing reads this metadata** - `stripe-webhook`
  matches work by `payments.stripe_checkout_session_id` - so it is forensic only,
  it may be truncated, and no settlement path may start depending on it.
- Checkout accepts at most 20 `line_items`. Above that, `fitLineItems()` groups by
  invoice KIND and unit price and puts the count in Stripe's `quantity` (grouping
  by label merges nothing: fifteen pledge spots have fifteen different labels).
  The per-invoice `payments` rows and the itemised receipt are untouched, so the
  ledger still breaks the payment down invoice by invoice.
