## 5f. Position papers and study guides: the Documents page (16 Oct 2026, prompt 129)

`/manage/[slug]/documents` is ONE page with no tabs (Overview, For everyone and Settings are gone). From 1100px wide it fits the viewport with no page scroll: the header, then two EQUAL columns (prompt 131). Every card is a Financials `DashCard` (the 3D emoji in its white-ringed disc hanging over the top-left corner, a larger title, an "i" with one sentence). LEFT, "Position Papers and Study Guides" (Scroll): six committees in view with NO inner scroll, ONE FULL-WIDTH ROW per committee, FIVE to a page since prompt 139 (prompt 133, owner: "6 rows ... not 6 squares"), the six rows sharing the card's height (left to right: the logo or MonogramMedallion, the short name with the full name beneath, wrapping and never cut, the three status lines stacked as a small icon and a short word, whose full sentence is the tooltip and accessible name: "Study guide published" / "Study guide releases {date}" / "Study guide not published" (a committee with no guide never shows a release date), "Position papers enabled" / "disabled", "Due {date}", then "View submissions" and "Edit" side by side, which open the committee's pop-up; below 1100px the rows keep their order and the buttons may wrap under the names), with "Page 1 of N" and arrows at the foot. RIGHT, "Things to Do" (Bell) and "Additional Documents" (File folder), stacked, sharing the column's height. Below 1100px the columns stack and the page scrolls. ONE pop-up per section: nothing on this page opens a pop-up on top of a pop-up (§8). `?committee=<id>` (with `&pane=settings`) opens a committee's pop-up on the page that holds it; `?tab=everyone&doc=<id>` still reaches a document's returned copies.

- **The committee pop-up** (`documents/CommitteeDocsPopup.tsx`, on the Settings pop-up shell `AwardsSheet`): the committee's logo and short name, tabs Submissions and Settings. Submissions is the position paper roster moved unchanged (counts, the All / Submitted / Reviewed / Approved / Rejected filters, `PositionPaperRoster` with Approve and Reject), with "Position paper submission is not yet enabled. Enable them here." while submissions are off. Settings: **Study Guide** (upload, the guide rows with Delete, and "Study Guide Release Date" with the line "Delegates see it once you set a release date" / "Released on {date}" / "Releases on {date}") and **Position Papers** (the switch "Open position paper submissions", and "Position Paper Deadline" while it is on; off keeps the saved deadline). **"Copy from another committee"** (top right, the roles' CopyFromMenu pattern) copies the release date, the switch and the deadline in one call, never the guide file; with nothing to copy it stays visible and says "No other committee has settings yet". Its menu stays inside the pop-up (one line per committee: logo, short name, a short summary with "No study guide" for a committee without one; its own scroll; it opens upward when there is no room below) and the copy is confirmed in the pane. "Upload study guide" turns the Settings tab into the upload form (Title, PDF, Cancel, Upload) in the same pop-up; Cancel or a finished upload returns to Settings with the guide listed, and the page's card updates when the pop-up closes.
- **One write: `save_committee_documents(p_committee_id, p_patch)`** (organizers; any of `pp_submissions_enabled`, `position_paper_deadline`, `study_guides_publish_at`), a verified write: the committee it returns is applied, a refusal keeps the old value and shows its sentence under the field. It switches the conference to per-committee settings, so the old "same for every committee" mode and its controls (`set_conference_pp_deadline`, `set_conference_sg_publish`) are no longer used by the page.
- **A study guide follows its release date only.** Saving a release date sets `is_published` false on that committee's guides, so there is no Publish or Unpublish: a date in the past means released now, a date moved into the future re-arms the "Study guide released" email.
- **The right column (prompt 130).** **Things to Do** (`documents/DocumentsTodo.tsx`) lists, oldest first, what waits for an organizer's decision from `documents_todo(p_conference_id)`: position papers 'submitted' that no organizer has cleared ("{delegate}, {committee} position paper") and, only while docs_extra is on, copies sent back for an extra document ("{delegate}, {document}"), each with how long ago it came in, or "Nothing to review right now". A row opens the review pop-up (details and actions left, the file right, like the payment proof review): a position paper offers Accept, Reject and Let Chair Review through `organizer_review_position_paper(p_paper_id, 'accept' | 'reject' | 'chair', p_note)` (chair leaves the status alone and only takes it off the organizers' list; the chairs still review it); a returned copy offers Accept and Reject through `review_document_return`. A reject note is always optional ("Note for the delegate (optional)"); a position paper rejected WITH a note also sends it as a reviewer message in the paper's thread, the same thing a chair's feedback is. After a decision the pop-up moves to the next item, or closes with "All caught up"; the list re-reads when the pop-up closes, after the committee pop-up closes and on `gv-documents-returns-changed`. The card looks like the Financials Things to Do: a big number "N things to do", the rows, and "Review now"; nothing waiting is the green check tile "You're all caught up". **Additional Documents** (`documents/AdditionalDocuments.tsx`) shows the big number "documents shared", "View Documents" and "Add Document", and everything opens in ONE pop-up whose content changes: the list (name, file or link, who sees it, "{n} of {m} sent back" with the due date, Edit, View submissions on documents asking for copies back, the move arrows and Remove, confirmed on the row; "Add Document" top right), the add flow (`DocumentPopup embedded`; step 1 has "Back to Documents" bottom left; from the page card it opens straight on the flow and its "Back to Documents" shows the list; after Upload the list shows), Edit (the flow on Review, "Back to Documents" above it) and View submissions (`DocumentReturnsPopup embedded`, "Back to Documents", Submitted selected and waiting copies first).
- **Add Document is a five-step flow** (`documents/DocumentPopup.tsx`, "Step 1 of 5", Back and Continue, the document on the right from step 2): 1 "Upload Your Document" (a file, PDF, Word, JPG or PNG up to 10 MB, uploaded on Continue, or a link), 2 "Name and Description" (no kind picker any more: new documents are kind 'extra' with docs_extra on, 'other' on the classic path), 3 "Who Sees It?" ("Show to applicants under review too", off by default = accepted_only; the roles with Select all; Secretariat is no longer offered, and a document that already has it keeps it; every role selected saves the empty list = everyone), 4 "Ask Them to Send It Back?" (docs_extra only: the switch, the due date and Required; a link cannot be asked back), 5 "Review" with Edit links and "Upload Document" / "Save Changes". There is no email step: a NEW document is saved with p_notify true, an edit with false, and the "Document shared" / "Document to send back" emails are on by default and edited in Communications under Before the Conference. Edit opens the flow on Review. With docs_extra off the classic direct write is still the save path (and a saved document's file or link is not swapped).
- **Resubmitting is always allowed.** A participant can send a new copy of a document even after it was accepted ("Send a new copy"; it goes back to 'submitted'), and a delegate can always replace a position paper.
- **A replaced position paper always goes back to submitted.** A database trigger puts a paper back to 'submitted' and clears its review whenever its file changes, so a rejected or approved paper can be replaced and comes back for review.
- **Prompt 139 (19 Oct 2026).** Five committees per page. A row never crushes the name: the short name stays on one line and is never broken inside a word, the full name wraps under it only between words, and the name column keeps a minimum width; on a narrower card the status lines move under the name (the buttons stay beside it), and on the narrowest everything stacks. A committee whose position papers are disabled shows no "Due ..." line anywhere (the row, the copy view); the saved deadline is kept, so switching papers back on shows it again. Every date on the page and its pop-ups carries its year in one format ("Due 26 Sep 2026", "Releases 1 Dec 2026", "Released 22 Jul 2026"; `formatDate` in `CommitteeDocsPopup.tsx`, and en-GB in Things to Do and Returned copies). While the committee pop-up shows the Upload Study Guide form it shrinks to it (600px wide, its height from the content) and returns to its size on Cancel or Upload. **"Copy from another committee" is a view in the same pop-up** ("Copy Settings From"): every other committee as a card with its logo, short and full name and every setting written out (the study guide's title or "No study guide", "Releases / Released {date}" or "No release date", position papers open or closed, and the due date only while open); a click selects one (a green outline and a check), "Back" returns to Settings and "Copy settings" (off until one is chosen) makes the same `save_committee_documents` call, returns to Settings and says "Settings copied from {SHORT}".
- **Prompt 141 (19 Oct 2026).** A REJECTED position paper can always be replaced, whatever the committee's submission switch or deadline says (owner, 16 Oct): the participant's Documents section shows a primary "Upload and Replace" button under the file row, which opens the same upload; "Position paper submissions are not yet open for your committee" now only applies to a FIRST paper while submissions are closed, and the quiet "Replace" stays for papers that are not rejected while submissions are open. While a committee's papers are closed there is no "Due {date}" and no "Late" anywhere (the participant card, the paper page, the chair's and the organizer's rosters); the saved deadline is kept. The paper page draws the PDF with pdf.js on plain white (`src/components/documents/PlainPdfPages.tsx`): just the pages, lazily, at 75% of the page width (or the column's width when that is smaller), with round zoom out / zoom in buttons and the percentage beside Download (50% to 200%, steps of 25%); a file it cannot draw says "This file could not be shown here. Use Download to open it". The chair's review and the organizer's review pop-ups already used the site's pdf.js `PdfViewer`.
- **Prompt 142 (19 Oct 2026).** The tiny "Replace" link is gone: whenever a participant can replace their position paper, the card shows the conference theme's primary button "Upload and Replace" under the file row. It shows for a rejected paper always, and for a paper submitted or under review while submissions are open and the deadline (if any) has not passed; it is hidden after the deadline (unless rejected) and once the paper is accepted (a later rejection brings it back). A rejected paper goes straight to the upload; any other paper first confirms that the new version reopens it for review. The paper page opens signed storage URLs in pdf.js: `isPdfJsUrl` (the one test every pdf.js viewer asks) accepts this project's public AND signed object URLs (`/storage/v1/object/sign/...?token=`), with file names plain or encoded, still refusing dot segments and encoded slashes. Before this, signed URLs failed the test: the paper page said "could not be shown" and PdfViewer silently used the browser frame. The role page cards Position Paper, Study Guide and Questions and Requests (and Awards, Your Co-Delegate) use the "Conference Documents" card style: a bold 19px title (`CardHeading` / `CARD_TITLE_STYLE`), 15.5px item titles, 13 to 14px body, the same padding; the old small gold capitals eyebrows are gone. Add Document: a file the preview cannot draw (Word, or an image that fails to load) shows its name, type and size with a "Download" button on the right of every step, and a link shows "Open"; in View Documents a link row has "Open" and a Word row "Download". A file over 10 MB of an allowed type now says "The file is over 10 MB. Make it smaller and try again" (the type check used `isAllowedDocFile`, which also refuses large files, so a big PDF read as the wrong type). For a link, step 4's send-back switch carries a small lock and stays off, and "To ask people to send it back, upload the file itself, not a link" sits in a yellow warning box at the bottom of the left column, above Back and Continue.
- **Prompt 143 (19 Oct 2026).** Every document that is a file can be downloaded. A file the browser can show (PDF, image) has "Open" and "Download" side by side on the right of its row; a Word file has "Download" only; a link has "Open" only. This holds on the participant's Conference Documents card (both the classic and the extra card), the organizer's View Documents list, the participant's own sent copy ("View" and "Download"), the organizer's copy review (`ReturnFileViewer`: Download for Word, "Open full size" and Download for an image, the PDF viewer's own download for a PDF), the position paper rows (the participant's card, the chair's and the organizer's rosters) and the paper page (desktop and phone). "Download" is a real download with the file's own name: `downloadStoredFile` / `downloadConferenceDoc` (`src/lib/conferenceDocuments.ts`) and `downloadPositionPaper` (`src/lib/positionPapers.ts`) make a signed URL with storage's download option and click it through a hidden link in the same tab, never a new tab. "Required" is a small red tag (the "Rejected" red) at the top right of the document's card, above Open and Download, no longer in the "Send it back by" line. A document can always be sent again: `my_conference_documents` returns `can_send_back` true for an accepted copy too, and the card shows "Send a new copy" then. The send-back pop-up checks the file type before the size, so a large allowed file gets the 10 MB sentence.
